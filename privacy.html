MyPick

Privacy Policy

A simple privacy policy for a simple idea: play together, without giving us your personal data.

Effective date: 7 September 2026

1. Overview

MyPick is designed as a local party and arcade game. In the current version of the app, you do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information to us in order to play.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, and we do not use the app to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. Information stored on your device

Some information may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences and scores. This may include:

  • game settings and visual theme preferences;
  • local high scores and leaderboard entries;
  • a name or nickname you choose to enter for a local leaderboard.

This information is stored locally by the app and, in the current version, is not intentionally transmitted to us.

3. Data we collect

MyPick does not currently operate a user account system, cloud leaderboard, advertising platform, or developer-run analytics service.

Accordingly, we do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, payment information, or gameplay history on our own servers through the current version of MyPick.

4. Apple and device services

Your use of MyPick takes place on an Apple device and may involve services provided by Apple, including the App Store, iOS, crash diagnostics, or other device-level services that you have enabled. Information handled by Apple is subject to Apple's own privacy policies and settings.

MyPick does not control Apple's independent collection or processing of that information.

5. Children

MyPick does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into local leaderboard name fields. A nickname or first name is sufficient.

6. External links

The app or this website may contain links to third-party services, including Apple. Once you follow an external link, the privacy practices of that third party apply.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local settings and leaderboard information remain on the device until they are reset within the app, removed by deleting the app, or otherwise cleared by the operating system or user.

Because the current version does not intentionally send this information to our servers, we generally do not hold a server-side copy to delete.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if MyPick changes, for example if a future version introduces online services, analytics, accounts, or cloud leaderboards. If we make material changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
