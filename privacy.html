Privacy Policy
Last updated: 17 September 2026

Our Approach to Privacy

MyPick is designed to be a simple, fun game for friends, families and groups. We keep data collection to the minimum necessary to provide the app's features.

MyPick does not require you to create an account and does not use advertising, advertising tracking or analytics services.

Information MyPick Uses

Most MyPick gameplay takes place directly on your device. Game selections, touch interactions and other normal gameplay information are not sent to us.

Games that produce a leaderboard score use both a Local Leaderboard and a Global Leaderboard.

Local Leaderboards

Local leaderboard information is stored on your device. It is used to display your local scores and rankings within MyPick.

Local leaderboard information remains on your device unless the same qualifying score is also submitted to the Global Leaderboard as described below.

Global Leaderboards

When you record a qualifying score in a MyPick game that supports leaderboards, MyPick automatically submits that leaderboard result to the Global Leaderboard.

The information submitted is limited to:

  • Your leaderboard display name
  • The game identifier
  • Your score or reaction time
  • The date and time the leaderboard result was submitted

If you do not enter a leaderboard name, the result is submitted and displayed using “No Name”.

Do not use your leaderboard name to share information that you do not want other MyPick users to see.

Public Leaderboard Information

Global Leaderboards are a public feature within MyPick. Leaderboard display names and qualifying scores may be visible to other people using the app.

MyPick does not require your real name. You may use a nickname or leave the name blank, in which case “No Name” will be displayed.

Firebase and Cloud Firestore

MyPick uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to operate its Global Leaderboards.

The leaderboard information described above is transmitted to and stored using Google's Firebase infrastructure so that Global Leaderboard results can be displayed across devices.

MyPick does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication or Firebase Advertising features.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: Google Privacy Policy .

What We Do Not Collect

MyPick does not intentionally collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your postal address
  • Your contacts
  • Your precise location
  • Your photos or videos
  • Health information
  • Payment or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking

MyPick does not require an account or social-media login.

Advertising and Tracking

MyPick contains no third-party advertising.

We do not use your MyPick activity to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

How Leaderboard Information Is Used

Global Leaderboard information is used only to provide and operate MyPick's leaderboard features, including displaying scores and rankings to MyPick users.

We do not use leaderboard information for advertising or marketing profiling.

Data Retention

Global Leaderboard entries may remain stored in Cloud Firestore so that leaderboard rankings can continue to operate.

Local leaderboard information can be reset using the controls provided within MyPick. Resetting a Local Leaderboard does not automatically delete an entry that has already been submitted to the Global Leaderboard.

Removing a Global Leaderboard Entry

If you would like a Global Leaderboard entry removed, please contact us using the support contact details below.

Please provide enough information for us to identify the entry, such as the leaderboard display name, game and approximate score or reaction time.

Children's Privacy

MyPick is designed as a general-audience game and does not require users to create accounts or provide their real names.

Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information as a Global Leaderboard display name.

Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the information collected by MyPick and to protect information used by the Global Leaderboard.

Access to MyPick's Cloud Firestore database is controlled using database security rules designed to restrict the information that the app can read and submit.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if MyPick's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be changed accordingly.
